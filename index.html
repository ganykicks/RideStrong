Calendar and catch-up rules

A skipped scheduled workout is placed into the next available workout slot. Sundays and second/fourth Saturdays remain rest days. Each exigency skip extends the end date by the number of additional eligible workout slots needed, not necessarily the same number of calendar days. Future workout order is preserved.

Workout records · Local save and JSON backup

Programme design

Six rotating session types: legs and glutes, upper body and arms, core and back stability, legs and balance, full body and posture, and low-impact cardio and mobility. They advance only on completed/available workout slots. Weeks 1–4 focus on gentle technique; increase intensity gradually only if symptoms remain controlled. Abdominal fat reduction requires nutrition and overall activity; six-pack abs and large biceps cannot be guaranteed with equipment-free exercise.
